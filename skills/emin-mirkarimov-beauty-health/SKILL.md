### 2. Engineer Precision Medicine, Longevity, and 100% Effective Nutrition

1. **Calculate Exact Energy and Macronutrient Stoichiometry:**
   - Compute Basal Metabolic Rate (Mifflin-St Jeor or Katch-McArdle when lean mass is known) and Total Daily Energy Expenditure (TDEE).
   - Set caloric targets with mathematical precision: `-350 to -500 kcal/day` for sustainable fat loss without metabolic adaptation, or `+200 to +300 kcal/day` for lean muscle accretion.
   - Lock daily protein at `1.6 to 2.2 g/kg` of target body weight (`2.4 g/kg` during aggressive cutting), ensuring `3.0 g+` of leucine per meal across 3 to 4 feedings.
   - Set dietary fats at `0.8 to 1.0 g/kg` (prioritizing monounsaturated olive/avocado lipids and marine EPA/DHA Omega-3s) and allocate remaining calories to low-glycemic, fiber-rich complex carbohydrates timed around training.
2. **Optimize Glycemic Control, Gut Microbiome, and Chrononutrition:**
   - Sequence meals to flatten glucose spikes: eat fibrous vegetables first, protein and fats second, and starches/sugars last, followed by a 10-minute post-prandial walk.
   - Target `30+ diverse plant species` per week plus polyphenol-rich and fermented foods (`kefir`, `kimchi`, `sauerkraut`) to maximize microbiome diversity and short-chain fatty acid (butyrate) production.
   - Align feeding with circadian biology: consume the majority of calories in an `8 to 10 hour` daytime window and finish the last meal `3 hours` before sleep.
3. **Evaluate Longevity Biomarkers and Evidence-Graded Supplementation:**

### 3. Direct Elite Personal Training, Biomechanics, and Periodization

1. **Program Hypertrophy and Strength Periodization:**
   - Structure training splits around recovery capacity (Upper/Lower 4x, Push/Pull/Legs, or Full-Body 3x) delivering `10 to 20 hard sets` per muscle group per week.

   - Enforce double-progression overload (increase reps within target range first, then add `2.5% to 5%` load) and schedule a reactive or proactive deload week every 4 to 6 weeks.
2. **Integrate Cardiorespiratory Longevity (Zone 2 + VO2 Max):**
   - Prescribe `150 to 240 minutes/week` of Zone 2 aerobic training (nasal breathing / conversational pace at `65-75% Max HR`) to build mitochondrial density and metabolic flexibility.

3. **Correct Posture and Joint Kinematics:** Counteract modern desk posture with thoracic extension, scapular retraction (face pulls, lower-trap Y-raises), deep core bracing, and hip-flexor/glute balance.
