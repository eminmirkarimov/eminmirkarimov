# Creative Production, Engineering & Cultural Intelligence Bible

## 1. Local vs. International Cultural & Market Intelligence Matrix

Use this comparative framework whenever designing websites, apps, posters, ad creatives, video edits, or brand strategies for local vs. foreign markets:

| Dimension | Local / Regional Markets (High-Context: Baku, Caucasus, CIS, MENA) | Foreign / International Markets (Low-Context: US, EU, UK, Global SaaS/Media) | "Glocal" Masterpiece Synthesis (Best of Both Worlds) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Visual Status & Aesthetics** | High appreciation for visible craftsmanship, rich contrast, deep obsidian/gold/warm bronze accents, authoritative portraits, tangible luxury. | Preference for "Quiet Luxury", Swiss editorial grids, negative space, understated matte tones, candid documentary realism. | Use Dark Room / Swiss precision layout with rich cinema lighting and tactile specular highlights without visual clutter. |
| **Trust & Conversion Triggers** | Direct personal communication (WhatsApp/Telegram CTA), founder/team authority, video testimonials, clear phone/location presence, VIP service feel. | Self-serve instant onboarding, transparent pricing tiers, SOC2/GDPR badges, G2/Trustpilot metrics, interactive product demos. | Pair frictionless self-serve UI with an instant concierge/messenger action and verifiable social proof. |
| **Video Pacing & Audio Mood** | Strong emotional melody, dramatic bass drops, dynamic speed-ramps, charismatic hero shots, hospitality and prestige storytelling. | Fast 0.8s–1.5s narrative hook, clean sound design (foley + minimal rhythmic bed), kinetic typography, problem-solution clarity. | Hook in 1.2 seconds with Hollywood foley, drive pacing with Bezier speed-ramps, and blend modern electronic/cinematic stems with subtle cultural motifs. |
| **Photo Curation Psychology** | Polished posture, strong jawline, dignified composure, flawless wardrobe grooming, celebratory warmth. | Candid micro-expressions (Duchenne laugh), natural daylight imperfections, dynamic movement, approachable eye contact. | Select frames with authentic Duchenne eye warmth and impeccable anatomical posture; retouch via Dodge & Burn while keeping 100% real skin pores. |

## 2. Layout & Art Direction Archetypes (`taste-skill` & `frontend-design`)

- **The Pinned Poster:** One fixed architectural focal element while asymmetric editorial columns scroll past.
- **Colour Chapters:** Full-viewport sections where background surface tokens shift intentionally per narrative act.
- **The Dark Room:** Deep obsidian/carbon surfaces (`#090A0C`) where products, cinema frames, or photography glow via controlled specular highlights.
- **The Quiet Instrument:** High-precision Swiss typography, monospace technical metadata, generous negative space, and tactile micro-interactions.
- **The Specimen Catalogue:** Bento and edge-to-edge hairlines (`1px solid rgba(255,255,255,0.08)`) presenting items like curated museum artifacts.

## 3. Hollywood Color Grading Presets & Node Specifications (Kodak 2383 Formula)

- **Lift (Shadows):** Cool cyan-blue bias in deep shadows (`R: -0.015, G: -0.005, B: +0.020`), neutral black point at 0% IRE.
- **Gamma (Midtones):** Warm organic push toward the vectorscope I-line (`R: +0.012, G: +0.004, B: -0.010`).
- **Gain (Highlights):** Creamy rolled-off warm highlights (`R: +0.018, G: +0.010, B: -0.025`), soft knee starting at 75% IRE.
- **Hue vs Hue:** Shift pure blues slightly toward teal/cyan (`-12 deg`), push yellow-greens toward deep emerald foliage (`-18 deg`), align orange-red toward cinema skin warmth.
- **Sat vs Lum:** Desaturate deepest 10% shadows and brightest 15% specular highlights (`Lum vs Sat` bell curve) so blacks and whites remain pure.

## 4. Foreign-Studio Editing & Sound Design Blueprint

- **Speed-Ramp Curve:** `100%` real-time entry -> `450% - 800%` apex across 4-7 frames (`180-270 deg` shutter angle) -> steep left-leaning Bezier ease-out (`85%` influence) landing on the beat transient.
- **Audio Mixing & Mastering Chain:** High-pass dialogue at `80 Hz`, notch `250-350 Hz` (`-2.5 dB`), sidechain music bed at `1.2-3.5 kHz` (`-3 dB` pocket), combine 3 SFX layers (Pre-Roll Riser `-18 dB` + Transient Hit + `40-65 Hz` Sub Drop), and master to `-14 LUFS` (Web) or `-23 LUFS` (Cinema) at `-1.0 dBTP`.

## 5. Psychological Photo & Frame Selection Matrix (100-Point Scoring)

- **Micro-Expression & Emotional Authenticity (30 pts):** Genuine Duchenne smile or composed authoritative gaze, zero blink/squint asymmetry, relaxed lip corners.
- **Anatomical & Pose Flattery (25 pts):** Clean jawline shadow, elongated neck posture, natural hand tension (soft fingers, never rigid claws), asymmetrical shoulder line.
- **Visual Attention & Eye-Tracking (20 pts):** Sharp focus on the nearer eye, clear specular catchlight, face luminosity `0.5 to 1.2 EV` brighter than background distractions.
- **Color & Tonal Depth (15 pts):** Clean skin-to-background color contrast, absence of green/magenta color cast on skin shadows.
- **Cultural & Brand Resonance (10 pts):** Aligns with local hospitality/prestige cues or international editorial minimalism.
