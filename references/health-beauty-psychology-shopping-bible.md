## 2. 100% Effective Nutrition & Body Recomposition Protocol

1. **Lean Body Mass (LBM) Estimation:** `LBM = Weight (kg) * (1 - Body Fat %)`.
2. **BMR (Katch-McArdle):** `BMR = 370 + (21.6 * LBM)`.
3. **Target Calories:**
   - **Precision Fat Loss (100% Muscle Preservation):** `TDEE - 400 kcal` (`0.5% to 0.8%` body weight loss/week).
